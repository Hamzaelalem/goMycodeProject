# CLIENT ESG Policy Targets & Framework — 2026

## Policy Overview
CLIENT's ESG framework is aligned with the UN Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs), the Paris Agreement, and the IFC Performance Standards. The portfolio-level ESG score is computed quarterly using sector-specific KPI inputs weighted against these targets.

## Portfolio-Level ESG Targets

### Environmental Targets
- Portfolio carbon intensity: Reduce by 40% vs. 2022 baseline by 2030
- Renewable energy exposure: Minimum 35% of portfolio AUM by 2027
- Zero coal exposure: Maintained since 2021
- ESG Environmental Score threshold: Portfolio average ≥ 70/100
- Water treatment efficiency: Portfolio companies average ≥ 85%

### Social Targets
- Financial inclusion: Portfolio banking companies to serve minimum 2M unbanked customers
- Local employment: Minimum 60% local staff at portfolio company management level
- Community investment: Minimum 2% of portfolio company EBITDA to community programs
- ESG Social Score threshold: Portfolio average ≥ 65/100
- Gender diversity: 40% women in portfolio company boards by 2027

### Governance Targets
- Board independence: Minimum 40% independent directors at portfolio companies
- Anti-corruption: Zero-tolerance policy — immediate portfolio exclusion on violation
- ESG Governance Score threshold: Portfolio average ≥ 75/100
- Regulatory compliance: Zero material regulatory violations
- Audit quality: Big-4 or equivalent auditors required for investments >$50M

## Sector-Specific ESG KPI Weights

### Solar & Energy
- Environmental: 50% weight (carbon credits, LCOE, renewable %)
- Social: 30% weight (community energy access rate)
- Governance: 20% weight (regulatory compliance score)
- Overall ESG grade thresholds: A+ ≥ 88, A ≥ 80, B+ ≥ 72, B ≥ 64, C ≥ 50

### Oil & Gas
- Environmental: 45% weight (flaring intensity, spill incidents, methane leakage)
- Social: 30% weight (local employment %, safety incidents)
- Governance: 25% weight (reserve reporting accuracy)
- Note: Oil & Gas portfolio is subject to enhanced ESG monitoring due to transition risk

### Banking
- Environmental: 25% weight (green loan portfolio %, paper usage)
- Social: 40% weight (financial inclusion index, employee diversity)
- Governance: 35% weight (NPL ratio, capital adequacy)
- Enhanced governance weighting reflects banking sector systemic risk

### Hospitals
- Environmental: 20% weight (medical waste management score)
- Social: 50% weight (patient satisfaction, staff-to-patient ratio)
- Governance: 30% weight (clinical governance score)

## ESG Score Calculation
Overall ESG Score = (E Score × E Weight) + (S Score × S Weight) + (G Score × G Weight)
Letter Grade: A+ (88-100), A (80-87), B+ (72-79), B (64-71), C (50-63), D (<50)

Current Portfolio ESG Score: 69/100 (Grade: B+)
Target by Q4 2026: 75/100 (Grade: B+, approaching A)

## ESG Exclusion Criteria
CLIENT maintains a negative screening list. Automatic exclusion for:
- Thermal coal production (>5% revenue)
- Tobacco production or distribution
- Cluster munitions or landmine manufacturing
- Companies with active FCPA or equivalent enforcement actions
- Companies with more than 2 material ESG violations in 36 months
